01 / About me

How I got here.

I turn messy data into something a machine can learn from, and find the signals buried in it. Fraud and anomaly detection is where that counts most: the cases that matter are rare and usually look perfectly normal. Looking closer until they give themselves away is the part I find most exciting.

My first master's was about analyzing data, and after working with it every day, I noticed the part I enjoyed most was building things and understanding how they work under the surface. So I followed that. At the ECB I worked as a business analyst and data engineer before moving into backend development. Then I went back to university for a second master's in Data Science and AI at TU/e, specializing in machine learning to reskill for the field I want to work in. Now I'm doing an internship at ING, getting a close look at how a bank builds and checks the models it relies on to fight financial crime.

Having built the pipelines and APIs that machine learning models run on, I've seen what happens end to end: data validation, testing, deployment on AWS and monitoring in production. On the modeling side, I enjoy the whole cycle of building predictive models: framing the problem, engineering features, choosing how to evaluate them and understanding where they fail. I want to work where those two halves meet.

Curiosity is a better compass than a plan.

Anti-money laundering detection
